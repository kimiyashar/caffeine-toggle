# Caffeine Toggle 3.0.1

This update fixes Custom schedule activation in the compact menu-bar panel.

## Fixed

- Custom schedules can now be enabled when their selected days and times happen to match the Weekdays or Every Day presets.
- The app remembers that those schedules are Custom after the panel closes or the app restarts.
- Existing advanced recurrence schedules remain preserved and toggle-only in the compact editor.

## Download and install

1. Download **Caffeine-Toggle-3.0.1.zip** from the Assets section below.
2. Open the ZIP and drag **Caffeine Toggle** into **Applications**, replacing the prior version.
3. On first launch, Control-click the app and choose **Open**.

No Xcode or Terminal is required. This build supports Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later.
